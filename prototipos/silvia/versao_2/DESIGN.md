## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a dialogue between specialized nephrological precision and intimate, empathetic medical consultation. Designed for Dr. Silvia Pimenta's private practice in Florianópolis, it bridges scientific authority and maternal warmth, removing clinical intimidation while elevating medical excellence.

The design movement balances **Warm Minimalist Editorial** with **Organic Tactility**:
- **Medical Authority & Grounding:** Deep sapphire-night navy provides unwavering scientific credibility, clinical governance, and diagnostic confidence.
- **Biophilic Renewal:** Restorative eucalyptus and sage tones symbolize renal balance, cellular health, clean filtration, and life renewal.
- **Human Connection & Empathy:** Gentle terracotta and antique rose nuances—inspired by tactile knitwear and authentic personal care—infuse compassion, dedicated listening, and approachable comfort into patient touchpoints.
- **Tactile Comfort:** Alabaster, warm linen, and pearl-cream surfaces replace sterile, fluorescent clinical whites, reducing white-coat hypertension and creating a serene sanctuary for patients navigating acute and chronic renal care.

## Colors

The chromatic system is anchored by four thematic pillars reflecting human physiology, restorative nature, and clinical rigor:

- **Primary (`#0F2942` - Sapphire Night Navy):** Imparts medical prestige, diagnostic clarity, and structural authority. Applied to primary CTAs, main headlines, navigation anchors, and dominant branding.
- **Secondary (`#2E6B65` - Deep Eucalyptus / Sage):** Connects to fluid balance, renal vitality, and biological restoration. Governs secondary actions, reassurance panels, diagnostic status cues, and medical credential tags.
- **Tertiary (`#C47B6A` - Soft Terracotta / Medicinal Antique Rose):** Directly attuned to personal warmth and sensitive listening. Used for empathy callouts, testimonial borders, decorative badge highlights, and gentle notification accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F5` Alabaster Linen) & Container (`#F3EFEA` Warm Pearl):** Softens the visual field, mitigating sensory fatigue and clinical anxiety for both patients and elderly family members.
- **Named Color (`#25D366` - Direct WhatsApp Conversion):** Reserved strictly for patient triage, fast-track scheduling, and immediate clinic receptionist contact via WhatsApp.

### Hierarchy & Functional Logic
- Text high-emphasis: Deep Charcoal Slate (`#16212B`) over `#FAF8F5` achieves high contrast and AA/AAA accessibility.
- Text medium-emphasis: Slate Grey (`#4E5862`).
- Borders and Dividers: Muted Warm Sand (`#E5DFD7`).
- Active Highlights: `#C47B6A` on hover accents, subtle underlines, and quote indicators.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between editorial warmth and accessible clinical legibility:

- **Editorial Serifs (`Playfair Display`):** Elevates headings, pull quotes, personal statements from the physician, and welcoming section titles. The subtle inclusion of italic styles adds intimate resonance without sacrificing clinical credibility.
- **Functional Humanist UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Ensures readability across all age groups, including elderly patients and caregivers reviewing lab guidelines, treatment plans, and appointment instructions. Its generous apertures and tall x-height guarantee effortless scanning.
- **Overlines & Microcopy:** Styled in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with tracked uppercase (`letterSpacing: 0.06em`) to structure medical classifications, subheadings, and clinic locations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a fluid 12-column responsive grid structure centered within a maximum width of `1200px` for desktop viewports, ensuring content never feels crowded or overly wide.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` canvas margins. Asymmetric layouts pair a 5-column physician narrative with a 7-column clinical service or booking module.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` screen margins.
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` horizontal margins. Interactive components span full-width for effortless single-hand thumb navigation.
- **Rhythm & Calm:** Generous vertical section padding (`space-xl` and above) introduces visual breathing room, creating an unhurried, reassuring consultation experience.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth follows an **Ambient Warmth & Soft Glass** methodology, avoiding sterile harsh drop-shadows:

- **Base Layer (Canvas):** Flat, soothing `#FAF8F5` providing a tranquil organic foundation.
- **Tonal Container Layer:** `#F3EFEA` used for nested consultation information, intake requirements, and background sections.
- **Raised Clinical Cards:** Surface `#FFFFFF` layered over the linen background, bordered with a delicate `1px` stroke in `#E5DFD7`, supported by an extra-diffused, warm-tinted ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 30px -4px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.05)`.
- **Floating Modals & Urgent CTAs:** Translucent alabaster glass (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.90)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) framed by a subtle terracotta border tint (`rgba(196, 123, 106, 0.30)`), cast with a soft shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -8px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.10)`.
- **Interactive State Elevation:** Hovering over actionable cards increases shadow blur radius smoothly without sharp displacement:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(46, 107, 101, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary employs organic, rounded geometry to mirror human biology and alleviate the sharpness common in medical software:

- **Buttons and Inputs:** Standard radius of `0.5rem` (8px), delivering gentle yet structured touch targets.
- **Cards and Containers:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) creates soft framing for diagnostic descriptions, treatment plans, and physician profile modules.
- **Featured Hero & Portrait Visuals:** `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) softens the doctor's portrait, clinical setting snapshots, and patient testimonial cards.
- **Badges, Pills & Status Tags:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) provide friendly, non-punitive signaling for clinical specialties, certifications, and operational hours.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Agendamento de Consulta):** Deep Navy background (`#0F2942`), pure white text, `0.5rem` border radius, padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Hover transitions to a softened deep navy with a subtle terracotta focus indicator (`#C47B6A`).
- **Secondary Action (Conheça os Tratamentos / Exames):** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in Eucalyptus Sage (`#2E6B65`), text `#2E6B65`. Hover state applies `rgba(46, 107, 101, 0.06)` background fill.
- **Direct WhatsApp Action:** Solid `#25D366` with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` text, bold weight, accompanied by the official messaging icon. Focus ring: `rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.35)`.
- **Terracotta Empathy Action (Espaço do Paciente / Cartilha):** Soft terracotta fill (`#C47B6A`), white text, designed for educational downloads and supportive patient guides.

### Cards
- **Clinical Service Cards (e.g., Tratamento de Doença Renal Crônica, Litíase Renal, Hipertensão):** Built on `#FFFFFF` with `1rem` corner rounding, fine border in `#E5DFD7`, and soft ambient elevation. Hover transitions tint the top border with `#2E6B65` (2px accent).
- **Empathy & Testimonial Cards:** Feature a light alabaster background (`#F3EFEA`) with a warm terracotta quotation mark accent and `Playfair Display` italic excerpt typography.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E5DFD7` border, `0.5rem` corner radius, and comfortable `0.875rem 1rem` padding.
- Focus state activates an empathetic halo: `border-color: #2E6B65; box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(46, 107, 101, 0.15)`.
- Error validation replaces red with a respectful terracotta-crimson tone (`#B84A39`), preserving dignity while ensuring clarity.

### Chips & Badges
- **Credentials & Specializations (CRM-SC, RQE Nefrologia):** Pill silhouette (`9999px`), background `rgba(46, 107, 101, 0.08)`, text `#2E6B65`, uppercase `12px` with `0.06em` tracking.
- **Acolhimento & Humanized Indicators:** Pill silhouette, background `rgba(196, 123, 106, 0.12)`, text `#C47B6A`.

### Lists
- Clinical bullet points and preparation instructions use bespoke check icons or soft botanical leaves tinted in `#2E6B65` instead of standard browser bullets.
- Generous list item spacing (`space-sm` to `space-md`) ensures high accessibility when reviewing exam guidelines.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Rounded square (`4px` radius) and circular selectors framed in `#E5DFD7`, animating to a filled `#0F2942` or `#2E6B65` with a warm white indicator icon upon selection.

### Custom Component: Care Guide Accordion
- Designed for nephrological questions (e.g., creatinina, hemodiálise, dieta renal). Unfolds with a warm sand divider, displaying clear `Plus Jakarta Sans` body typography without visual clutter.
