## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-tier specialized medical care, precision clinical authority, and empathetic warmth. Designed for an elite nephrology practice situated in Florianópolis, the interface merges high-end private medical hospitality with clinical rigor.

The aesthetic philosophy balances **Editorial Luxury** and **Warm Minimalism**:
- **Authority & Longevity:** Deep renal blues anchored by rich editorial serif typography deliver a sense of grounded science, clinical excellence, and dependable counsel.
- **Serenity & Healing:** Restorative sage greens and coastal sand tones replace sterile clinical whites, cultivating psychological ease for patients managing chronic or acute kidney health concerns.
- **Tactile Refinement:** Subtle champagne metallic accents, gentle glass surfaces, and generous spatial margins evoke the atmosphere of an exclusive seaside wellness sanctuary rather than a standard institutional hospital.
- **Human Connection:** Clean data visualization and uncluttered layouts ensure medical information remains accessible, respectful, and transparent.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from kidney vitality, calm waters, and coastal warmth:

- **Primary (`#17384E`):** Deep Renal Navy. Used for authoritative headline elements, dominant brand anchors, primary action states, and high-contrast clinical highlights. Its deeper step (`#0E2433`) provides profound depth for footers, active navigational states, and high-emphasis banners.
- **Secondary (`#2C6E6E`):** Restorative Sage / Deep Teal. Represents cellular renewal, renal health stability, and organic calm. Used for secondary actions, interactive badges, clinical reassurance cards, and supportive iconography. Its lighter nuance (`#3D8D88`) provides gentle hover and focus rings.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37`):** Subtle Champagne Gold. An accent reserved for delicate borders, active indicators, clinical certification accents, and discreet luxury cues. Pair alongside warm sand (`#EAE5D9`) for structural borders and dividers.
- **Neutrals (`#FDFBF7` canvas, `#F4F6F8` surface container):** Luxurious, warm off-white tones that eliminate clinical harshness while preserving WCAG AAA compliance for body text (`#1E293B`).

### Functional Color Applications
- **Success / Healthy Markers:** `#2C6E6E` (aligned with restorative secondary tone rather than neon greens).
- **Warning / Alert:** `#C07D32` (warm amber gold) ensures urgent lab notifications stand out without panic.
- **Error / Critical:** `#9E2A2B` (deep crimson brick) maintains elegance during validation feedback.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy builds an unmistakable balance of medical prestige and effortless clarity:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for hero titles, editorial section anchors, medical philosophy statements, and doctor profile introductions. It imparts an unmistakable literary distinction, communicating bespoke personal attention and academic authority.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Chosen for its rounded geometric precision, high x-height, and superior legibility across long medical explanations, appointment scheduling forms, and lab report panels.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Rendered in small caps or upper-case tracking with `Plus Jakarta Sans` to structure patient portals, diagnostic test classifications, and clinic credentials cleanly.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adheres to an airy 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (max content container `1240px`) shifting to a 4-column structure on mobile devices:

- **Air & Tranquility:** Generous negative space between editorial modules prevents patient sensory overload, instilling calm confidence from the first touchpoint.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Clinical narratives, specialist credentials, and treatments utilize alternating asymmetric column offsets (e.g., 5 columns text + 7 columns photography or consultation cards) to maintain visual curiosity.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Desktop (>= 1024px)`: 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `3rem` section margins.
  - `Tablet (768px - 1023px)`: 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, `2rem` section margins.
  - `Mobile (<= 767px)`: 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` screen padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Soft Glassmorphism** layered with **Tonal Sand Surfaces** and **Warm Ambient Shadows**:

1. **Base Tier (Canvas):** Flat, soothing `#FDFBF7` without synthetic starkness.
2. **Layer 1 (Card & Clinical Panels):** Surface `#FFFFFF` with ultra-fine `1px` borders in tinted sand (`#EAE5D9` with 80% opacity) accompanied by an ambient, diffused shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 4px 24px -2px rgba(23, 56, 78, 0.05)`.
3. **Layer 2 (Floating Booking Overlays & Modals):** Translucent frosted glass effect using `background: rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px);` framed by an exquisite champagne accent border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`).
4. **Interactive Hover Elevation:** Smooth transitions shifting the shadow to `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(23, 56, 78, 0.09)`, subtly lifting cards without harsh displacement.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates intentional soft curvature to evoke empathy, gentle human anatomy, and clinical approachability:

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** Feature an `8px` (`0.5rem`) radius, providing structural neatness with gentle edges.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg`):** Feature a `16px` (`1rem`) border radius, soft on the eyes and framing medical content gracefully.
- **Featured Consultation Modules (`rounded-xl`):** Feature a `24px` (`1.5rem`) corner radius for hero imagery, testimonial panels, and doctor portrait frames.
- **Avatars & Status Pills:** Fully rounded / pill shapes (`9999px`) used for tags, certifications, and active navigation indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Consultation / Agendamento):** Background `#17384E`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `8px`, with a subtle `1px` inset ring in `#0E2433`. Hover state transitions to background `#0E2433` with a soft gold focus indicator (`#D4AF37`).
- **Secondary (Learn More / Exames):** Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #2C6E6E`, text `#2C6E6E`. Hover fills with `rgba(44, 110, 110, 0.08)`.
- **Tertiary / Champagne Link:** Text `#17384E` with an animated underline accent in `#D4AF37`.

### Cards & Clinical Blocks
- Structured on pure white or subtle warm sand surfaces (`#F4F6F8`) with `16px` corners.
- Cards host a subtle top border highlight of `2px solid #D4AF37` on featured services (e.g., *Check-up Renal Avançado*, *Manejo da Hipertensão*).

### Input Fields & Selects
- Form inputs for consultation bookings and telemetry inputs feature background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #EAE5D9`, text `#17384E`, and `14px` vertical padding.
- Focused state applies an ethereal halo: `border-color: #2C6E6E; box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(44, 110, 110, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Badges
- Medical tags (e.g., *CRM-SC*, *RQE Nefrologia*, *Atendimento Personalizado*) utilize a pill form (`9999px`), background `rgba(44, 110, 110, 0.10)`, text `#2C6E6E`, and font weight `600`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square (`6px` radius) and circular controls with a neutral `#EAE5D9` boundary, filling with `#17384E` and an ivory checkmark when selected.

### Specialized Component: Clinical Credential Badge
- An editorial badge featuring the gold seal of medical boards, styled with an inset border (`#D4AF37`), subtle warm background (`#FDFBF7`), and refined label typography.
