## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a serene, deeply empathetic, and refined medical presence tailored specifically for domestic veterinary care. It bridges the warmth and tenderness pets receive inside their homes with the uncompromising technical rigor of clinical veterinary medicine.

The visual aesthetic sits at the intersection of **Organic Warmth** and **Modern Soft Minimalism**:
- **Tonal Comfort:** Moving away from sterile, harsh clinical whites into tranquil herbal greens, soft warm creams, and delicate coral-rose touches derived directly from the brand insignia.
- **Humanized Authority:** Clean geometry paired with smooth, approachable contours to ensure pet parents feel immediate emotional reassurance while trusting the practitioner's clinical expertise.
- **Calm Atmosphere:** Generous negative space, airy layouts, and gentle ambient depth that reflect an unhurried, stress-free veterinary consultation at home.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional balance between botanical vitality, compassionate tenderness, and clinical authority:

- **Primary (`#4EAE68` - Soft Sage):** Evokes healing, vitality, and nature. Used for key interactive drivers, primary buttons, active states, and reassurance indicators.
- **Secondary (`#F89090` - Warm Coral):** Echoes the tender heart motif of the brand. Serves as an empathetic accent for badges, priority highlights, tags, and micro-interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#1B382B` - Deep Forest):** Provides editorial contrast, clinical grounding, and crystal-clear readability for typography and high-emphasis icons.
- **Neutral (`#FAF9F6` - Canvas Cream):** Replaces harsh stark whites with a tactile, tranquil linen-like background, ensuring a warm domestic feel across large surface areas.

### Functional Roles
- **Canvas Base:** Always utilize `#FAF9F6` as the foundational background.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** Elevated cards rely on pure `#FFFFFF` resting upon `#FAF9F6`, creating subtle, natural layered depth without muddy shadows.
- **Text Hierarchy:** Headings use `#1B382B`, secondary body copy uses `#5C7368`, and muted captions utilize `#879C93`.
- **Accents & States:** Soft tint states (`#EAF5EE` and `#FDF2F2`) house icons and category badges to keep visual noise minimal.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the friendly, organic geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the hyper-legible neutrality of **Inter**.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings & Labels):** Delivers a welcoming, modern feel with rounded interior terminals that directly reflect the organic line-art of the logo. Used for all titles, statistics, badges, and button labels.
- **Inter (Body & Content):** Provides clean rhythmic clarity for clinical advice, procedure descriptions, FAQs, and medical explanations, ensuring effortless legibility even on small mobile screens.
- **Optical Weighting:** Headlines should use medium to bold weights (`600` - `700`) rendered in Tertiary Deep Forest (`#1B382B`) to maintain grounding and dignity, avoiding harsh black.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a relaxed, rhythmic 12-column responsive fluid grid with maximum content containment at `1200px` to maintain comfortable eye-tracking distance.

- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column grid, `gutter: 1.5rem`, outer section margin `margin-desktop: 3rem`. Vertical section spacing utilizes `space-3xl` (`6rem`) to ensure breathability and an unhurried visual cadence.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid, `gutter: 1rem`, section margins `margin: 2rem`. Vertical section rhythm compresses to `space-2xl` (`4rem`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-sm: 1rem`. Information cards stack into single columns with internal component padding set to `space-md` or `space-lg`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layer stacking** accented with soft, diffused **ambient botanical shadows**. Harsh dropshadows and heavy dark borders are avoided to preserve a gentle domestic feel.

- **Surface Level 0 (Base):** Canvas Cream (`#FAF9F6`), completely flat.
- **Surface Level 1 (Resting Cards & Panels):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a whisper-light shadow tinted by the brand's deep forest tone: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 56, 43, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(27, 56, 43, 0.02)` and a soft perimeter border `1px solid rgba(78, 174, 104, 0.12)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Modals):** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 56, 43, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(27, 56, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Contact / Floating CTAs:** A gentle pill float with `0 8px 24px -2px rgba(78, 174, 104, 0.25)`.
- **Glass Accents:** For sticky navigation bars, use `rgba(250, 249, 246, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a bottom border `1px solid rgba(228, 236, 231, 0.8)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Rounded (`2`)** philosophy, echoing the continuous organic line-art of the cat, dog, and house outline in the brandmark:

- **Buttons, Badges & Chips:** Smooth pill forms (`9999px` border-radius) or generous rounded curves (`1rem`) that encourage touch and evoke approachability.
- **Cards & Content Containers:** Default to `1rem` (`16px`) radius, creating a comfortable framed presentation. Large hero highlight cards and authority banners scale up to `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Input Fields:** Styled with `0.75rem` (`12px`) border-radius, maintaining harmony between card surfaces and control elements.
- **Avatars & Image Masks:** Soft squircle or full circular frames (`50%`) with gentle sage perimeter rings (`2px solid #EAF5EE`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (WhatsApp / Consult Schedule):** Filled pill button with Soft Sage (`#4EAE68`) background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), medium-bold Plus Jakarta Sans typography. Hover shifts to a richer forest green (`#3D9455`) accompanied by a subtle transform scale (`translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary Action (Learn More / Service Details):** Crisp white surface with a soft boundary (`1.5px solid #4EAE68`), sage text (`#4EAE68`), and hover fill of Pale Sage (`#EAF5EE`).
- **Emergency / Urgent Triage Link:** Soft Warm Coral fill (`#F89090`) or gentle outline with dark forest label, indicating special healthcare caution without creating panic.

### Cards (Services & Authority)
- **Structure:** Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`), `1rem` radius, bordered by hairline `#E4ECE7`.
- **Header:** Accompanied by a circular icon badge (`48px × 48px`) in Pale Sage (`#EAF5EE`) housing a Soft Sage (`#4EAE68`) stroke icon.
- **Body:** Plus Jakarta Sans headline (`18px`), Inter body text (`15px`), followed by a subtle bulleted checkmark list using Sage checkmarks.

### Badges & Chips
- **Category Chips:** `roundedness: 3` (pill), padding `6px 14px`, text `label-md`.
- **Status & Trust Indicator (e.g., "CRMV-SC 10332", "5.0 no Google"):** Pale Sage container with deep forest copy and golden/coral star accents.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Field Base:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E4ECE7`, padding `14px 16px`, text `Inter 16px`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#4EAE68` with an outer soft glow ring `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(78, 174, 104, 0.18)`.
- **Radio & Checkboxes:** Custom rounded selectors; checked state uses `#4EAE68` with crisp white check indicators.

### Accordions & FAQs
- Expandable panels enclosed in white cards with soft borders. Active state reveals an answer block bordered internally by a subtle sage left-accent line (`3px solid #4EAE68`).

### Trust & Testimonial Sliders
- Testimonial cards feature an open quote glyph in soft coral (`#F89090` at 20% opacity), a warm, human review snippet, and pet/owner identification tags with rounded avatar thumbnails.
